Privacy Policy

How Textery handles analytics, subscriptions, AI prompts, and the fictional stories you create in the app.

Last updated: April 1, 2026

Textery ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect limited information needed to provide and improve the app:

  • Usage Data: Analytics about app usage patterns, feature usage, and subscription events to improve the product.
  • Content You Create: Chat stories, characters, and messages are stored locally on your device. We do not treat your story library as hosted account data.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status is managed through Apple and RevenueCat. We do not store your payment card details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Textery service
  • To improve app quality, product decisions, and stability
  • To understand feature usage and troubleshoot issues
  • To process subscriptions and premium access

Data Storage

Your stories and characters are primarily stored locally on your device using Apple's storage mechanisms.

When you export a video, rendering may be processed temporarily on our servers and deleted after delivery. We do not keep a hosted library of your exported videos.

Third-Party Services

Textery uses third-party services to operate key features:

  • Aptabase: privacy-focused analytics
  • RevenueCat: subscription management and entitlement handling
  • OpenAI / Anthropic: AI story generation based on prompts you provide

Children's Privacy

Textery is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete locally stored app data by uninstalling the app. Subscription management is available through your App Store account settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@kimbytes.com.
