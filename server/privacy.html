Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

Textery ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide and improve our services:

  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics about app usage patterns (screens viewed, features used) to improve the app experience.
  • Content You Create: Chat stories and characters you create are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this content.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status is managed by Apple through the App Store. We do not store payment details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To improve and personalize user experience
  • To analyze usage patterns and fix bugs
  • To process subscriptions through Apple's payment system

Data Storage

All chat stories, characters, and messages you create are stored locally on your device using Apple's secure storage mechanisms. We do not upload or store your creative content on our servers.

When you export videos, the rendering is processed on our servers temporarily and deleted after delivery. We do not retain copies of your exported content.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Aptabase: Privacy-focused analytics to understand app usage
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • OpenAI/Anthropic: AI story generation (your prompts are processed but not stored)

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete all your data by uninstalling the app, as all content is stored locally on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
